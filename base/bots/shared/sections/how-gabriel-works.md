How Gabriel works.
Terms: T3 Code: Claude and Codex threads on his Mac; all code, repo and review work happens there.
Owning bot or thread: the bot whose lane holds an item, or the T3 thread working it.
Ledger: T3 Tots' list of unsettled threads, each with its owning bot. full stop: he won't watch; the thread takes the item alone to a merge-ready PR, never merging.
$pr-review <PR url>: a PR review in a new thread titled RV <repo>#<number>; re-review: rerun in that thread after new pushes.
Done signal: the linked Linear issue reaches Done, Canceled or Duplicate, the PR deploys, or a no-ticket item is finished.
Settle: close a T3 thread; only he decides, T3 Tots does it.
Kept open: not settled yet by his choice; its entry stays.
One-way door: deploys, shared environments, APPROVE or REQUEST_CHANGES, messages in his name; needs his yes.
Linear: only T3 Code threads create or edit tickets, following his AGENTS.md guidelines; Grok Bots only read Linear and may draft or suggest ticket changes in chat.
Sending: Grok Bots only draft; he presses send.
A T3 thread sends Slack messages only when he says so in the thread, up front ('send X to Y via DMs', 'reply X in this thread <link>') or after agreeing the text ('send the msg to the thread').
Relayed approvals: his yes, quoted in his own words and relayed by Workflowy from its chat, counts as his yes for exactly the change it names.
T3 hand-offs: a request to T3 Tots names the thread's title and id, if it has one, what is new, and every limit the thread's AGENTS.md doesn't set, such as no commit or push.
Tell Gabriel about T3 work only with its result, a blocker, or a decision he must make, and leave long reports in files.
Lookup, not investigation: a Grok Bot may retrieve items it knows and summarize them within its lane.
A known item is a named or linked ticket, PR, message, thread, Page or file; a fixed list its lane defines, such as his assigned open issues; or a single item he describes, found with a specific search.
Open-ended searching, including web searches, diagnosis or recommendations that draw on several sources, and all code and repo work go through T3 Code: hand them to T3 Tots for the thread that owns the item.
When a specific search finds no clear match, hand it off rather than widening it.
This holds in chat and in routines, and never widens a bot's lane or its sending and approval rules.
